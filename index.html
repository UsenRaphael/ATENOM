Guides

Learn ATENOM in minutes.

  1. Press Open app to reach your dashboard.
  2. Click ⚙ Categories and add the kinds of things you spend on.
  3. Press + Add, then enter a name, amount, category and date.
  4. Open Reports to see where your money goes.
  1. Click ⚙ Categories on the Expenses page or the Budget card.
  2. Use + Add category, type a name and pick a color. You can have up to 20.
  3. Rename or recolor any row. Expenses follow the rename automatically.
  4. Deleting a category also deletes its expenses and budget, after you confirm and press Save.
  1. Click the Monthly budget card in the menu.
  2. Enter a limit for each category. Leave blank for no limit.
  3. Your monthly budget is all the limits added together.
  4. A bar turns yellow at 75% and red at 100%. Budget left shows what remains this month.
  1. Open Reports and choose a period, from This month to All time.
  2. The trend chart switches between daily and monthly bars on its own.
  3. Hover any bar or slice for the exact amount.
  4. The Total spent card compares with the previous period. Budget vs actual shows this month.
  1. Open Expenses and apply any category or period filter you like.
  2. Press Export CSV. The file holds exactly what you see.
  3. Open it in Excel, Google Sheets or any spreadsheet app.
  4. Your records live in your browser, so export regularly. Clearing browser data erases them.
  1. Press ✎ on a row to edit it, then Save changes.
  2. Press ✕ to delete. An Undo button appears for a few seconds.
  3. Search from the top bar, filter by category or period, and sort by date, amount or name.

About ATENOM

Know where your money goes.

ATENOM is a simple, fast expense tracker. Log what you spend, group it in your own categories, set budgets and see the story in clear reports. You don't need an account to start.

Open the dashboard Read the FAQ
🏷️

Your own categories

Groceries, data, church, fuel, school fees. Create the categories that match your life, pick a color for each, and rename or remove them any time.

🎯

Budgets that warn you

Set a monthly budget per category. The bars turn yellow at 75% and red when you pass 100%, so you see trouble before it arrives.

📊

Reports that explain

See spending over time, a breakdown by category, your biggest expenses and how this month compares with the last.

How it works

  1. 1
    Create your categories. Click ⚙ Categories and add the kinds of things you spend on.
  2. 2
    Log your expenses. Press + Add, enter a name, amount, category and date. Edit or undo a delete whenever you need.
  3. 3
    Read the story. Open Reports to see trends, category shares and how your budgets are holding up.

Private by default

Right now everything you enter is stored in your own browser on this device. Nothing is sent to a server. That also means clearing your browser data removes your records, so use Export CSV on the Expenses page to keep a copy. Accounts and syncing across devices are planned for later.
